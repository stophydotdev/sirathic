# Hajj and Umrah trip

Plan a Hajj or Umrah trip end to end: the permits and visa, the flights, the stay, and what to do each day, with every rite taken from cited sources. The flight_booking, accommodation_booking and restaurant_booking skills do the bookings when they are in your list; load them for those steps.

## Entry points

- "Plan an Umrah for my parents in the last ten nights of Ramadan" gives a season and the travelers.
- "Book us a Rawdah slot on Thursday" is one permit.
- "What do I do on the 8th of Dhul Hijjah?" is a rites question for one day.
- "Find a Hajj package from the UK for next year" is a regulated route.

## Before you start

- Pin down Hajj or Umrah, the travelers (the country of each passport, ages, women traveling, mobility), the dates or season, the departure city, the budget, and the order of the cities (Makkah first or Madinah first).
- The rules change every season: who may book Hajj and where, the Umrah visa types, the Nusuk permits for Umrah and the Rawdah, and age and vaccine rules. Read the official sources each time (nusuk.sa, the Saudi visa platform and the traveler's own Hajj authority) with fetch_url or your browser, and give the date of what you read. Never rely on memory.
- Hajj is booked through Nusuk Hajj for some countries and through a national Hajj body or licensed operators for others. Find which applies to their passport before you search for packages, and warn that unlicensed Hajj packages are a known scam.

## Flow

1. Build the itinerary first: the days in each city, Umrah on arrival or after Madinah, and for Hajj the 8th to the 13th of Dhul Hijjah. Turn Hijri days into dates with islamic_dates when it is in your tools, and say they are expected dates.
2. Find the miqat the route crosses before the flight is booked. For a Makkah-first trip by air, ihram is put on before the plane crosses it. Search which miqat applies to their route and how airlines announce it, and cite it.
3. Do the bookings with the booking skills: flights into JED or MED, and a stay near the Haram with the walking time to the gate.
4. Permits: book a Rawdah or Umrah slot in Nusuk with your browser when the user has an account; their sign-in goes through request_browser_help. Report the slot, the date, the gate and what to bring.
5. Write the day-by-day plan: each day's rites and their steps from search_web results, cited, with the views of the schools where they differ and the user's madhab first when known. Add prayer_times for Makkah or Madinah on those dates with the UmmAlQura method.
6. Offer once to save the plan as a file, and to add the bookings and permit times to their calendar.

## Pitfalls

- Stating a rite, a condition or a penalty (dam) from memory instead of a cited source.
- Assuming last season's visa or permit rules.
- Choosing a stay by straight-line distance; give the walking time to the Haram gate.
